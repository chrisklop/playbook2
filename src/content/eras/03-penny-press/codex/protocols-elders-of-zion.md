The *Protocols* were forged by agents of the Okhrana, the Tsarist secret police, most likely under Pyotr Rachkovsky, the Paris-based head of Okhrana foreign operations. The text first appeared serialized in the St. Petersburg newspaper *Znamia* ("The Banner") in August and September of 1903, presented as the leaked minutes of a secret Jewish-Masonic plan for world domination.

It was a literal cut-and-paste job. Whole passages were plagiarized from Maurice Joly's 1864 anti-Napoleon III satire *Dialogue in Hell Between Machiavelli and Montesquieu*, with "Machiavelli" lightly find-and-replaced into "the Elders." Additional fictional material was lifted from Hermann Goedsche's 1868 novel *Biarritz*. Philip Graves of *The Times* of London publicly exposed the plagiarism in 1921 by laying the texts side by side.

The exposure changed nothing. The *Protocols* continued — and continue — to circulate in dozens of languages, reprinted by the Nazi state, distributed by Henry Ford in the United States, and recycled today in online conspiracy ecosystems. It is the canonical template for state-manufactured forgery laundered through religious and nationalist press as a "leaked secret meeting transcript," and the cleanest demonstration that disinformation, once seeded into mass culture, can outlive its own debunking by more than a century.
