# ANTIQUITY ERA: Disinformation, Propaganda, and Manipulation (~3000 BCE – ~500 CE)

## 1. Era Overview

Antiquity's information environment was overwhelmingly **oral, monumental, and ritual**. Literacy was confined to scribal castes (Mesopotamian é-dub-ba schools, Egyptian temple scriptoria, Roman elite), so most "media" reached the public through public readings, processions, festivals, gossip in the agora and forum, theatrical performance, religious oracle, and physical objects: steles, temple reliefs, victory columns, and — once minted — coins. There was no concept of independent journalism, no mass literacy to fact-check claims, and no fast cross-checking across geography. A ruler's inscription on a temple wall was the authoritative record, regardless of what had actually happened.

This created powerful enablers for disinformation: (1) **monopoly over the written record** by rulers and priesthoods; (2) **vast information latency** — by the time a contradictory account from a distant province arrived, the official version was already canon; (3) **religious framing**, where claims of divine endorsement (Ahura Mazda, Heaven, the Capitoline gods) were unfalsifiable; (4) **performative speech** in courts and assemblies, where rhetorical skill outweighed evidence; and (5) **portable propaganda** in the form of coinage, which let rulers push iconographic messages into every transaction across the Mediterranean.

## 2. Key Examples

### 2.1 The Legend of Sargon & the Naru Tradition (~2300 BCE, Akkad)
Sargon of Akkad's "autobiography" — describing his secret birth, river-basket exposure, and rise from gardener to king — is preserved on tablets composed centuries after his death as part of the *naru* genre: pseudo-royal inscriptions that retroject legitimating origin myths onto historical rulers. His grandson **Naram-Sin** also became the protagonist of later didactic propaganda (*The Curse of Agade*, *Cuthean Legend*) reshaping his reign for political-religious lessons. **Techniques: impersonation/forgery, mythic legitimation.** ([World History Encyclopedia](https://www.worldhistory.org/article/746/the-legend-of-sargon-of-akkad/); [Cambridge: The Two Steles of Sargon](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/iraq/article/abs/two-steles-of-sargon-iconology-and-visual-propaganda-at-the-beginning-of-royal-akkadian-relief/B6EC9540759BCABC1B825839533E71BC))

### 2.2 The Battle of Kadesh (1274 BCE, Egypt vs. Hittites)
Ramesses II fought a tactical draw against Muwatalli II — failed to take Kadesh, nearly lost his army — then plastered the *Poem of Pentaur* and the *Bulletin* across at least five major temples (Karnak, Luxor, Abydos, Abu Simbel, the Ramesseum), depicting himself single-handedly routing the Hittite army. Hittite cuneiform tablets discovered at Hattusa contradict the Egyptian account — the first known case of a propaganda claim being externally falsified by recovered enemy archives. **Techniques: repetition across channels, suppression of contradicting facts, heroic personification.** ([Wikipedia: Battle of Kadesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Kadesh); [The Archaeologist](https://www.thearchaeologist.org/blog/the-battle-of-kadesh-ramses-iis-propaganda-and-the-truth-from-hittite-texts))

### 2.3 The Behistun Inscription (~520 BCE, Persia)
Darius I seized the Achaemenid throne after killing Bardiya (Cambyses II's brother). He carved a 15m-tall trilingual cliff inscription (Old Persian, Elamite, Akkadian) declaring that "Bardiya" had actually been an impostor magus named **Gaumata**, whom Darius righteously slew on Ahura Mazda's behalf. The 19 simultaneous provincial revolts after Bardiya's death suggest the population believed Bardiya was real. **Techniques: discrediting (impostor narrative), conspiracy theory, multilingual reach, divine endorsement.** ([World History Encyclopedia](https://www.worldhistory.org/Behistun_Inscription/))

### 2.4 Mandate of Heaven (~1046 BCE, Zhou conquest of Shang)
The Zhou justified overthrowing the Shang by inventing the doctrine that Heaven (*Tian*) had withdrawn its mandate due to Shang kings' drunkenness, cruelty, and luxury. Codified in the *Book of Documents* (*Shujing*), the doctrine became the template for every subsequent Chinese dynastic transition — manufactured moral failings of the predecessor became the cause of revolution. **Techniques: retroactive moral discrediting, theological framing, normalization of regime-change narrative.** ([Wikipedia: Mandate of Heaven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandate_of_Heaven))

### 2.5 Themistocles & Athenian Ostracism (~480s BCE)
Excavations on the north slope of the Acropolis recovered a cache of ~190 ostraka all naming Themistocles — written in only **14 distinct hands**. Whether interpreted as voter-assistance "how-to-vote cards" or outright ballot stuffing, this is direct material evidence of coordinated influence operations in the world's first democracy. **Techniques: astroturfing, coordinated ballot manipulation.** ([Peter Sommer Travels](https://www.petersommer.com/blog/another-thing/ostraka))

### 2.6 Athenian Sykophants (5th–4th c. BCE)
With no public prosecutor, Athens incentivized private citizens to bring lawsuits — and *sykophantai* exploited this by filing frivolous suits to extort settlements or destroy rivals. Demosthenes prosecuted **Aristogeiton** (324 BCE) as a paradigmatic sycophant, "a savage beast preying upon citizens." The first institutionalized disinformation-for-profit ecosystem. **Techniques: weaponized litigation, character assassination, extortion.** ([Stoa: Sycophancy and Attitudes to Litigation](https://www.stoa.org/demos/article_sycophancy@page=all&greekEncoding=UnicodeC.html))

### 2.7 Octavian vs. Mark Antony (32 BCE)
The textbook case. Octavian illegally seized Antony's will from the Vestal Virgins and read it publicly, claiming it bequeathed Roman territory to Cleopatra's children and asked for an Alexandrian burial. He paired this with a coin/pamphlet campaign casting Antony as a drunken, effeminate slave to a foreign queen, reframing a civil war as a patriotic war against Egypt. **Techniques: leaked/forged documents, xenophobic othering, gendered smear, polarization.** Cassius Dio Book 50.25-26 preserves Octavian's pre-battle speech. ([World History Encyclopedia](https://www.worldhistory.org/article/1474/the-propaganda-of-octavian-and-mark-antonys-civil/))

### 2.8 Augustan Imperial Coinage (27 BCE onward)
After Actium, Augustus systematized coin-as-propaganda: every denarius carried curated imagery — *Pax*, *Victoria*, the *clipeus virtutis*, *CAESAR DIVI F* ("son of the deified one"), oak crowns for "saving citizens." The *Res Gestae Divi Augusti*, inscribed on his mausoleum and copied in provinces, was a first-person sanitized autobiography. **Techniques: ubiquitous branding, self-deification, omission (no mention of proscriptions or Antony).** ([Wikipedia: Propaganda in Augustan Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propaganda_in_Augustan_Rome))

### 2.9 Nero & the Great Fire (64 CE)
Facing rumor that he had set the fire himself to clear ground for the Domus Aurea, Nero blamed the Christians, a tiny and reviled sect. Tacitus (*Annals* 15.44) — writing ~50 years later and no friend of Christians — explicitly calls them scapegoats. **Techniques: scapegoating a marginal minority to deflect rumor.** ([Tacitus on the Christians, Livius](https://www.livius.org/sources/content/tacitus/tacitus-on-the-christians/))

### 2.10 Pagan Smears of Early Christians (1st–3rd c. CE)
Greco-Roman rumor accused Christians of **cannibalism** (misreading the Eucharist's "body and blood"), **incest** ("brothers and sisters" in love), **atheism** (rejecting state gods), and ritual infanticide. Apologists like Minucius Felix (*Octavius*) and Tertullian (*Apology*) had to systematically rebut them. The same blood-libel template would migrate to anti-Jewish polemic in the Middle Ages. **Techniques: deliberate misinterpretation of in-group language, moral panic, conspiracy of secret rites.**

### 2.11 Procopius' *Secret History* (~550 CE)
Procopius wrote glowing official histories of Justinian and Belisarius's wars (the *Wars*), then secretly composed the *Anekdota* portraying Justinian as a literal demon and Theodora as a depraved whore. Whether the *Secret History* is true, a personal vendetta, or itself a counter-propaganda piece is debated — making it the era's bookend case of **dueling propaganda layers around a single regime**. ([Wikipedia: Procopius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Procopius))

## 3. Key Techniques Established

1. **Monumental repetition** — carve/inscribe the same message across many sites so the official version is physically inescapable (Kadesh, Behistun, *Res Gestae*).
2. **Divine/cosmic legitimation** — wrap political claims in unfalsifiable theology (Mandate of Heaven, Ahura Mazda, *divi filius*).
3. **Discrediting the predecessor** — invent or amplify the moral failings of the prior regime to justify your own (Zhou on Shang, Darius on "Gaumata," Octavian on Antony).
4. **Scapegoating the outgroup** — deflect rumor onto a vulnerable minority (Nero/Christians, pagans/Christians).
5. **Weaponized legal/civic process** — turn institutions (Athenian courts, ostracism) into tools of targeted character destruction (sykophants, Themistocles ostraka).
6. **Portable iconographic branding** — coinage as the world's first mass-distribution medium for political imagery (Augustus onward).

## 4. Key Figures

- **Ramesses II (r. 1279–1213 BCE)** — the first king known to convert a battlefield draw into a totalizing victory narrative.
- **Darius I (r. 522–486 BCE)** — pioneer of the trilingual, empire-wide official narrative; the "impostor" smear template.
- **Demosthenes (384–322 BCE)** — both wielder and prosecutor of rhetorical disinformation; the *Philippics* are textbook polarization speeches.
- **Octavian / Augustus (63 BCE–14 CE)** — the era's master propagandist; integrated literature (Virgil, Horace), coinage, monumental architecture, and document leaks into a unified system.
- **Procopius of Caesarea (~500–565 CE)** — court historian and secret libeller, showing how the same author can run two opposing propaganda channels simultaneously.
