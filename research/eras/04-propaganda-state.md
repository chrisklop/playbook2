# THE PROPAGANDA STATE ERA: 1914–1945

## 1. Era Overview

World War I birthed the modern propaganda state. For the first time, industrialized nations confronted a problem industrialized warfare had created: how to extract sustained consent, money, manpower, and hatred from mass populations whose stake in the war was abstract. The British (Wellington House, 1914) and Americans (Committee on Public Information, 1917) built the first peacetime-staffed bureaucracies dedicated to opinion management, fusing journalism, advertising, and government for the first time. Veterans of these bureaus — Edward Bernays, Walter Lippmann, Carl Byoir — carried the techniques directly into 1920s commercial PR and political consulting, secularizing wartime persuasion as a permanent industry.

Three technologies compounded the effect: cheap halftone print (mass-illustrated tabloids and posters), 35mm sound film (newsreels, feature propaganda, the Riefenstahl aesthetic), and shortwave radio (live, intimate, borderless, and impossible to ignore in the home). By the 1930s, Goebbels could put a cheap state-subsidized Volksempfänger radio in nearly every German household.

What made totalitarian propaganda different was scale, monopoly, and integration. Nazi Germany, the USSR under Stalin, and Fascist Italy each fused a single party, the entire press, the education system, the arts, and the security apparatus into one persuasion machine. Dissent wasn't merely outargued — it was made physically impossible to publish, broadcast, or photograph. Propaganda became indistinguishable from the state itself.

## 2. Key Examples

**1. US Committee on Public Information ("Creel Committee"), 1917–1919.** Created by Wilson via Executive Order 2594 under journalist George Creel. Produced posters, films (e.g., *Pershing's Crusaders*), 75 million pamphlets, foreign-language press outreach, and the **Four Minute Men** — ~75,000 volunteer orators who delivered over 750,000 short speeches in 5,200 communities (reel-change intermissions in cinemas), reaching ~400 million listeners. First modern total-spectrum domestic propaganda campaign. ([Wikipedia: CPI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committee_on_Public_Information); [LOC](https://www.loc.gov/exhibitions/world-war-i-american-experiences/about-this-exhibition/over-here/surveillance-and-censorship/four-minute-men/))

**2. Wellington House / British War Propaganda Bureau, 1914–1917.** Run secretly by MP Charles Masterman from National Insurance HQ. On 2 September 1914, Masterman convened 25 leading authors (Conan Doyle, Kipling, H.G. Wells, Chesterton, Hardy) to write covert pro-Allied material. By 1916 had distributed ~7 million pamphlets in 17 languages. Primary target: bringing the neutral US into the war. ([Wikipedia: Wellington House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wellington_House))

**3. Bryce Report on Alleged German Outrages, May 12, 1915.** Official UK government report under Viscount James Bryce, documenting German atrocities during the 1914 invasion of Belgium. Translated into every major language. A textbook case of *real-events-plus-lurid-exaggeration*: modern scholarship (Horne & Kramer, *German Atrocities, 1914*) confirms several thousand Belgian civilians were in fact executed, but Appendix A's accounts of mutilated babies and systematic rape were largely uncorroborated. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committee_on_Alleged_German_Outrages))

**4. The German "Corpse Factory" (Kadaververwertungsanstalt), 1917.** British black propaganda claim that Germans rendered their own war dead into fats, glycerine, and soap. Spread via planted stories in *The Times* and *Daily Mail*. Brigadier-General John Charteris (British military intelligence) admitted inventing it in a 1925 New York speech. Dark legacy: when real reports of Nazi extermination camps reached Allied intelligence in 1942, JIC chair Victor Cavendish-Bentinck dismissed them as a recycled "corpse factory" lie — directly delaying response to the Holocaust. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Corpse_Factory))

**5. Edward Bernays — "Torches of Freedom," March 31, 1929.** Bernays, a CPI veteran and Freud's nephew, was hired by American Tobacco's George Hill to break the taboo on women smoking publicly. After consulting psychoanalyst A.A. Brill, he staged debutantes lighting Lucky Strikes during the NYC Easter Parade, pre-tipping the press to frame it as a suffragist gesture. Women's share of cigarette consumption rose from 5% (1923) to 12% (1929). The founding template of modern PR: manufactured pseudo-event as news. ([Wikipedia: Torches of Freedom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torches_of_Freedom))

**6. Walter Lippmann, *Public Opinion*, 1922.** Lippmann, a former CPI propagandist, articulated the era's intellectual framework: citizens cannot directly perceive the modern world and instead operate on a "pseudo-environment" built from media-supplied "stereotypes" (his coinage). He first used "the manufacture of consent" in *Liberty and the News* (1919). The book became foundational doctrine for both PR practitioners and their later critics (Chomsky/Herman lifted the phrase for *Manufacturing Consent*, 1988). ([Project Gutenberg full text](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/6456))

**7. Nazi Propaganda: Goebbels, Riefenstahl, Streicher, and the Big Lie.** Joseph Goebbels was named Reich Minister for Public Enlightenment and Propaganda on March 13, 1933, taking total control of radio, press, film, theater, and the arts. *Triumph des Willens* (1935), directed by Leni Riefenstahl, documented the 1934 Nuremberg Rally using innovative tracking shots, aerial cinematography, and choral montage — establishing fascist political aesthetics. **Der Stürmer**, Julius Streicher's weekly tabloid (1923–1945, peak circulation 600,000), specialized in pornographic antisemitic caricature, blood-libel revivals, and public display in glassed "Stürmerkasten" cases. The **Big Lie** ("große Lüge") was articulated by Hitler in *Mein Kampf* (1925, Vol. 1 Ch. 10), originally attributing the technique to Jews — and then deployed against them. ([Wikipedia: Triumph of the Will](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triumph_of_the_Will); [USHMM: Der Stürmer](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/tags/en/tag/der-sturmer))

**8. Soviet Photo Falsification and Lysenkoism.** Censors at the State Publishing House systematically airbrushed purged figures from photographs — the most famous being NKVD chief **Nikolai Yezhov**, removed from a 1937 photo with Stalin on the Moscow-Volga Canal and replaced with water. David King's *The Commissar Vanishes* (1997) catalogs hundreds. Parallel epistemological assault: **Lysenkoism**. Agronomist Trofim Lysenko, championed in *Pravda* and personally backed by Stalin, denied Mendelian genetics in favor of Lamarckian inheritance. After the August 1948 VASKhNIL session, genetics was effectively outlawed; ~3,000 biologists were dismissed, imprisoned, or executed. State propaganda overrode empirical reality. ([History.com](https://www.history.com/articles/josef-stalin-great-purge-photo-retouching); [Wikipedia: Lysenkoism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lysenkoism))

**9. Italian Fascism: Istituto LUCE and MinCulPop.** **Istituto Luce** (founded 1924) produced the *Giornale Luce* newsreel from 1927 — by decree screened compulsorily before every feature film in every Italian cinema. The **Ministero della Cultura Popolare** ("Minculpop," 1937) controlled press, radio, film, and publishing. The slogan *"Il Duce ha sempre ragione"* anchored the personality cult. ([Wikipedia: Istituto Luce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istituto_Luce))

**10. Imperial Japan — Cabinet Information Bureau and the Co-Prosperity Sphere.** The **Cabinet Information Bureau** (Naikaku Jōhōkyoku), established 1940, centralized media control over press, radio (NHK), and film. The **Greater East Asia Co-Prosperity Sphere**, term coined by Foreign Minister Hachirō Arita on June 29, 1940, framed Japanese conquest as Asian liberation from Western colonialism. ([Wikipedia: GEACPS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_East_Asia_Co-Prosperity_Sphere))

**11. US Office of War Information & *Why We Fight*, 1942–1945.** OWI established by Executive Order 9182 (June 13, 1942) under Elmer Davis. By 1943 every major Hollywood studio except Paramount submitted scripts to OWI for review. **Frank Capra's *Why We Fight*** series (7 films, 1942–1945), commissioned by Gen. George C. Marshall, was originally for troop indoctrination but received theatrical release; *Prelude to War* (1942) won the 1943 Academy Award. Heavy use of repurposed enemy footage (including Riefenstahl's) recut to indict its makers. ([Wikipedia: OWI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Office_of_War_Information))

**12. Axis Radio: Lord Haw-Haw and Tokyo Rose.** **William Joyce** ("Lord Haw-Haw") broadcast from Reichssender Hamburg starting 1939, reaching an estimated 6 million regular UK listeners; hanged for treason 1946. **"Tokyo Rose"** was an Allied catch-all for English-speaking female announcers on Radio Tokyo (NHK); the woman convicted in 1949 was Iva Toguri D'Aquino, an American citizen stranded in Japan; pardoned by President Ford in 1977. ([Wikipedia: Tokyo Rose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Rose))

**13. BBC vs. Reichsrundfunk and Sefton Delmer's Black Stations.** The BBC German Service broadcast from 1938, building credibility by honestly reporting Allied defeats. In 1941 Churchill consolidated UK black propaganda under journalist **Sefton Delmer** at the Political Warfare Executive. His stations — *Gustav Siegfried Eins* (1941, fake disgruntled-Nazi-officer station), *Soldatensender Calais* (for the Wehrmacht, broadcast via the high-power **Aspidistra** transmitter) — pioneered the modern "false flag" media station: pretending to be German, mixing accurate news with disinformation, smearing Nazi leadership with manufactured sex scandals. An estimated 40% of German soldiers listened. ([Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/history/fake-british-radio-show-helped-defeat-nazis-180962320/))

## 3. Key Techniques Established

1. **Total-spectrum state propaganda** — coordinated saturation of print, poster, film, radio, school curriculum, and live oratory by a single government bureau.
2. **Atrocity propaganda calibration** — pairing real wartime crimes with sensationalized invented details to maximize emotional response. Created the post-war "atrocity skepticism" that crippled response to genuine Nazi crimes.
3. **The manufactured pseudo-event / modern PR** — staged spectacles designed to generate apparently spontaneous news coverage (Bernays). Birth of public relations as a profession.
4. **Propaganda film as monumental aesthetic** — Riefenstahl's visual grammar (low-angle hero shots, choreographed crowds, aerial sweep, music-image montage) imitated for the rest of the century.
5. **The Big Lie** — Hitler's doctrine that a colossal falsehood, endlessly repeated, is psychologically more credible than a small one.
6. **Black propaganda / false-flag broadcasting** — covertly run stations posing as enemy-domestic outlets. Direct ancestor of every modern influence-operation impersonation.
7. **Photographic falsification of the historical record** — Stalin's airbrushing of purged figures.

## 4. Key Figures

- **George Creel** (1876–1953) — journalist; chair of the US CPI; author of *How We Advertised America* (1920).
- **Charles Masterman** (1873–1927) — Liberal MP; first director of Wellington House.
- **Edward Bernays** (1891–1995) — "father of public relations"; author of *Propaganda* (1928).
- **Walter Lippmann** (1889–1974) — *Public Opinion* (1922), *The Phantom Public* (1925).
- **Joseph Goebbels** (1897–1945) — Reich Minister for Public Enlightenment and Propaganda 1933–1945.
- **Leni Riefenstahl** (1902–2003) — *Triumph des Willens* (1935), *Olympia* (1938).
- **Julius Streicher** (1885–1946) — publisher of *Der Stürmer*; hanged at Nuremberg.
- **Trofim Lysenko** (1898–1976) — Soviet agronomist; weaponized state biology.
- **Sefton Delmer** (1904–1979) — British black-propaganda architect.
- **Frank Capra** (1897–1991) — director of US Army's *Why We Fight* series.
